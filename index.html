Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book in advance, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome during open hours, but booking ahead — online, by phone or on WhatsApp — guarantees your time slot and avoids waiting.

Is the clinic PMDC registered?

Yes. Dr. Hammad holds PMDC Permanent Registration as a Registered Dental Practitioner — you can view the license in the About section above.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring any previous dental X-rays, records or prescriptions if you have them, plus a valid CNIC. If it's a follow-up, mention your previous visit when booking.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes — for urgent pain, swelling or trauma, message us directly on WhatsApp for the fastest response and we'll fit you in as soon as possible.

How do I confirm or reschedule an appointment?

After booking you'll receive a confirmation by phone or WhatsApp. To reschedule, just reply on WhatsApp or call the clinic directly.
